# Parent and guardian accounts

Register at `/register/parent` using the parent or guardian’s name, email, phone, state, city and password. The form asks for Terms of Use acceptance and Privacy Notice acknowledgement. It contains no registration fields for children. Both legal pages remain labelled as drafts awaiting legal review.

Email confirmation activates the account and opens `/dashboard/parent`. The initiating browser can confirm its registration draft. A different browser asks the mailbox owner to re-enter their details and set a password. You can resend an expired verification link from the parent confirmation page. Older email-first verification links route to the same confirmation form. Verified parent accounts created before the profile fields were introduced can complete the profile after signing in.

## Account and request permissions

- Settings can change the signed-in parent’s name, phone, state and city. The verified email is displayed as read-only. Password reset uses shared authentication.
- Find Teachers and Digital Library link to the public areas of the site. Parent accounts have no access to restricted teacher profiles or documents.
- In **Request Lesson Teacher**, start a general request or open a specific tutor from Find Teachers. Record the subjects, education level, number of learners, objectives, lesson format, general location, preferred days and time ranges, and sessions per week. Qualifications and additional requirements are optional. The form asks parents to leave out learner identifiers and street addresses. Saving creates a private, editable draft.
- In **My Requests**, open your draft, review the saved information and submit it. The parent’s email must still be verified. If the draft changed since it was opened, review the current details before submitting. Repeating a successful submission keeps its timestamp and record.
- If the selected tutor becomes unavailable, edit the draft and choose **Continue as a general request** to keep the saved lesson details.
- Submitted details are read-only for parents. Staff review the request, match and shortlist tutors, and manage the arrangement. My Requests shows the current stage, parent-facing notes, and the confirmed teacher reference when available.
- Recommended Teachers shows currently available public tutors on the parent's active shortlists. Lesson Arrangements lists confirmed requests. Notifications shows tutoring status updates.

Profiles, request lists, details, edits and submissions all use the signed-in owner. A request ID from another account cannot reveal or change that request.
