# Managed candidate disclosures

Draft operational policy. Furoeducare still needs a legally reviewed policy, a documented approval, a published notice and a monitored privacy contact. Keep managed disclosures disabled until these are in place.

## Information, recipients and purposes

Furoeducare uses account and verification information to authenticate users, qualify participants and manage recruitment and tutoring. Candidate cases record the requester, purpose, requirements, any linked vacancy or tutoring request, selections, and staff decisions. Public listings use a limited set of professional fields. Teachers also supply private profile information, CVs and verification records for staff review. Parents and guardians should describe learning needs and scheduling without including learner names, street addresses or other identifying details.

Managed disclosures are restricted to the account that owns an eligible recruitment or tutoring case. Staff check that the case and teacher remain eligible, confirm the requester is acting in the correct school or parent/guardian role, and determine which information is necessary for the documented purpose. The professional scope uses a limited professional profile. Original CV access requires its own request and grant for the current reviewed CV, which can itself contain personal contact details and information about third parties. Identity and other verification files are excluded. The recipient may use the material only for the recorded case and purpose. The reviewed policy must set recipient obligations for confidentiality, onward sharing, local retention and deletion.

## Basis for a grant

The implementation supports independently obtained, case-specific documented consent. Before approving, authorised staff must independently confirm that the teacher freely and affirmatively agreed to this particular disclosure after receiving the applicable notice. The consent record must identify the recipient and case, the purpose, the exact scope and, for an original CV, the current reviewed document. It must also record the applicable notice version, date and method of consent, and how the teacher can withdraw. Store the record in the approved restricted evidence system and record only its reference in Furoeducare. Teachers must be able to decline without losing unrelated services. A signup acknowledgement, the Terms of Use, public listing, an eligibility decision and a staff approval do not provide the required consent evidence. [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, sections 24–27 and 35](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf), [General Application and Implementation Directive 2025, articles 15–18 and 27](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf).

Under the draft procedure, staff must also inspect an original CV for incidental information about other people or other fields beyond the approved purpose. Where the content cannot be shared within the reviewed policy, decline the original-file request and consider whether the professional profile scope meets the purpose. Legal and privacy reviewers must assess the lawful basis for other processing activities, any sensitive or child data, transfer arrangements, controller and processor roles, and whether an impact assessment is required before production activation. [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, sections 25 and 28–31, and 41–43](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf), [General Application and Implementation Directive 2025, articles 28 and 45](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf).

## Approval and activation

1. Furoeducare management and the responsible privacy and legal reviewers approve a versioned policy, the corresponding public notice, scope and eligibility criteria, evidence handling, recipient conditions, retention schedule and rights-handling procedure. The approval record identifies the decision, approvers, date, approved notice URL and exact version. Keep it in the restricted policy record.
2. The release owner confirms that the approved version is accessible at its public HTTPS URL and that the contact mailbox is monitored and routes requests to authorised staff. Use a separate published notice URL. The repository’s `/privacy-policy` page is a working draft and, once configured, links to the approved notice. The application checks configuration format. The release owner verifies publication, content and approval against the policy record.
3. Set `DISCLOSURE_POLICY_VERSION` to the approved version identifier, `DISCLOSURE_POLICY_APPROVAL_REF` to the approval record reference, `DISCLOSURE_PRIVACY_NOTICE_URL` to the approved HTTPS notice, and `DISCLOSURE_PRIVACY_CONTACT` to the monitored email address. Keep the disclosure policy version and approval reference unset if approval or publication is missing. A real privacy contact may be configured independently while the policy is in review.
4. On replacement of the policy, publish and activate a new version. Recipients must acknowledge that version and the reviewing administrator must reopen the decision form. Remove the activation configuration if the notice or handling process stops meeting the approved conditions. Revocation permanently ends a grant. If a policy is retired, revoke any remaining grants tied to it so reinstating configuration cannot restore access.

## Expiry, revocation and retention

The platform caps access at seven days for a professional profile and 24 hours for an original CV. Use one current grant per case, teacher and scope. Management may approve a new version of the material, which supersedes the previous grant for that case and scope. Expiry and revocation end authorised platform access. They cannot remove information a recipient copied, viewed or downloaded before access ended. When a teacher pauses sharing in `/privacy/controls`, the platform permanently revokes their current grants and prevents new grants until they resume. Resuming requires new case-specific approval and never restores revoked access. The teacher can see disclosure scope, purpose, recipient category and expiry or revocation in their private history.

The privacy owner and records custodian must approve and implement a schedule covering case records, profile snapshots, CV versions, consent evidence, staff decisions, privacy requests, downloads, audit records and backups. For each category, document the retention purpose, retention trigger, deletion or anonymisation procedure, backup handling and any applicable legal hold or minimum retention requirement. Grant expiry controls access. It does not schedule data deletion. The final periods and automated deletion procedures remain open pending review. [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, sections 24 and 27](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf).

## Privacy requests

Signed-in account holders can submit and track access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and consent-withdrawal requests in `/privacy/controls`. A monitored privacy contact covers people without account access and other matters, including portability and complaints. The published notice and approved procedure must explain how to contact Furoeducare and how to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, sections 34–38 and 46](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf), [General Application and Implementation Directive 2025, articles 36–40](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf).

Designated administrators triage the queue at `/dashboard/admin/privacy-requests`. They verify identity proportionately, involve the privacy lead for review and exceptions, track applicable response deadlines, and act across the affected records and recipients. Promptly disable further access for an applicable withdrawal or restriction and contact recipients as required by the approved procedure. Record the outcome only after carrying out the action or informing the requester of the reasons it could not be fulfilled. Users can read the response in their own account. Use the approved secure delivery method for copies of data and keep sensitive material out of request and resolution notes. Submitting a request starts staff review. Only the separate teacher pause control automatically revokes existing grants.
